# Rizing Tides ImpactOS™

## Development Intelligence Platform

ImpactOS is a cloud-based platform that enables organisations to manage, monitor, measure and report on development initiatives.

### Core Capability Areas

- Economic Development
- Enterprise Development
- SED Programmes
- Community Development
- ESG Management
- Monitoring & Evaluation
- Compliance Management
- Evidence Management
- Reporting Automation

### Modules

- Programme Management
- Beneficiary Management
- SMME Development Hub
- Compliance Centre
- Evidence Vault
- Reporting Studio
- AI Copilot
